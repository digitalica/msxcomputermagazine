   1) Introduction

      1.1) A brief history of the list

      When I become a member of Internet, in August 1993, I started looking
for MSX users who have e-mail. I have found some people in Brazil (using a
mailing list, ENECOMP-L) and a little group of MSX users on Chile. So, I have
started this list in order to collect e-mails as a reference to people
interested in exchanging ideas, softs and information.
      But, since January 1994, a MSX revolution become real on Internet. I
have become a member of CPM-L, a mailing list for CP/M users. I have sent the
(little) list to CPM-L, as I have done with ENECOMP-L. I was surprised when
lots of mails were dropped on my mailbox, most of them people from Holland,
wanting to exchange information, PD soft, demos, ALL about MSX! So, our list
become bigger and bigger. I started to communicate with BBS which are
connected with the Net, and a friendly SysOp (Lauro Faria - BDI BBS - Brazil)
sent it to FidoNet (a worldwide BBS network). So, the list become a really BIG
list (there is more than 320 users now). Nowadays there is people from Brazil,
Holland, Japan, Italy, Norway, Finland, Korea, Australia, etc., as you can see
above.

      1.2) Goals

         1.2.1) Goals which were achieved

         Despite this list, the Internet MSX users have 3 FTP sites: in
Finland (nic.funet.fi), in Russia (riaph.irkutsk.su) and in Japan
(ftp.saitama-u.ac.jp). It is important to note that the japanese site is
mirroring the first two sites. There is 3 mailing lists, too: a brazilian list
(where all posts are in Portuguese), an Japanese one - MSX-ML (where all posts
are in Japanese) and the international MSX users mailing list, the older,
bigger and most popular one.
         We have some World Wide Web (WWW or W3) areas too, who belongs to
Marat Fayzullin, Wiebe Weikamp, Tjipke Plaats, Maarten Bodewes, Alex Wulms and
Michel Meilink. We have 2 Usenet newsgroups: alt.sys.msx (which is an
alternative newsgroup - unfortunately isnt everybody who can read it), and
comp.sys.msx (the official newsgroup).


         1.2.2) Goals which will be achieved

      Next attempts - Another directories, maybe on ftp.luth.se or
ftp.sunet.se. Max Celedon (hydefus@inf.utfsm.cl) is doing contacts in order to
create this directory. My lab has received eight new workstations with more
than 10 Gb of HD, so I will moderate a FTP site here in Brazil soon.
      Near future - Contact more and more MSX users, at FidoNet and Internet.
So, we need to be connected! Possibly a electronic magazine (or a Web
Magazine), and a MSX FAQ. Alex Wulms (wulms@stpc.wi.leidenuniv.nl) is
collecting questions in order to create the FAQ. Please colaborate  with Alex,
sending questions  (and answers). We will need all help.
      Sky is the limit! - Create a fixed channel about MSX at IRC, and open a
directory at SimTel, the gigantic software depository.

      1.3) The future

      Surely this list become a revolution into the MSX world community,
connecting MSX users from different countries. As the moderator, I am happy
with these changes that this textfile has done in the world of MSX. We dont
know what we should expect from the future, but the future belongs to God.
Only He knows...

      2.2) CP/M sites on Internet

      As you may know, most CP/M programs can be used by MSX. Turbo Pascal
3, WordStar 3 (there is WordStar 4, which is PD) and our favourite
compressors, like PMARC, came from CP/M.
      The CP/M moderator (ftp://oak.oakland.edu/pub/cpm/), Jeff Marracini,
is doing a great work reindexing all the list (approximately 300 Mb of files),
looking for old Microsoft utilities, such as M80 and L80, helping to prepare
the CP/M FAQ, etc.
      A company called Walnut Creek has released a CP/M CD-ROM, with lots of
textfiles and maybe all the CP/M directory. So, there is many new files at
Internet for CP/M that we can use with MSX.

         CP/M main FTP sites:

   3) Lists

      3.1) MSX / CP/M list

      There is a CP/M mailing list on Internet. It is labeled CPM-L and if you
want to be a member,  follow these easy steps:

         - mail listserv@rpitsvm.bitnet
          (without subject)
           subscribe cpm-l <your name>

      As I have said above, there is a MSX mailing list. If you want to be a
member of the mailing list, proceed as the following steps above:

         - Send mail to majordomo@stack.urc.tue.nl, without subject.
         - In the body of the message: subscribe msx <your name>.

      You will be automatically subscribed to the MSX users mailing list, and
you should be prepared with enough space in your mailbox!
      In January of 1995, it was founded a Brazilian MSX-users mailing list.
If you want to be a member, you must know that all mails will be in
Portuguese, so, it may cause some problems to non-portuguese speakers. You
should proceed as described in these steps:

      Send a mail to:    esquina-das-listas@dcc.unicamp.br, without subject.
      Write              'inscreva msxbr-l <your e-mail>'.

      If you want to post mails, send a mail to the same e-mail adress, and
write in the first line: 'submeta msxbr-l'. After that, write down your
message.
      There is a Japanese mailing list, too. It is called MSX-ML, and if you
want to be a member, send a mail to <...> so you will be subscribed. Dont
forget, all posts are in Japanese.


      3.2) comp.os.cpm, and comp.os.misc and other Usenet newsgroups

      comp.os.cpm is a Usenet newsgroup where people talks about CP/M, and
comp.os.misc talks about other operating systems who dont have a specialized
newsgroup (MSX included). Unfortunately I dont know how to connect to these
newsgroups (you may use rtin or tin, but only if your server has a
subscription to a news server) so you should have them installed into your
Unix server. You can access them by email, which is efficient, but its slower
than rtin...
      Finally we have now two Usenet newsgroups, which names are alt.sys.msx.
and comp.sys.msx (the first is an alternative one, and the next, the
official), but I dont know to which news server are they linked. Im looking
for a GOOD Usenet-by-e-mail server, so as I have all info, I will put here. We
have opened an official newsgroup, comp.sys.msx. (Thanks to all the 152 YES
votes to the newsgroup). I think that while you are reading this textfile, the
newsgroup will be opened. Please read topic 1.2.1 for more information.

   MCX, from Chile, posted these ideas for the newsgroup(s):

   - comp.sys.msx.software - discussion about new software (demos, games,
     etc.), and old software's comments (reviews, translations, etc)
   - comp.sys.msx.hardware - discussion about the MSX hardware, including the
     news released and problem with them...
   - comp.sys.msx.programming - its name says all, special for coders (like
     me), programming's tricks, etc.
   - comp.sys.msx.misc - miscellaneous stuff...

   Maybe we can prepare an reorganization soon... ;-)
